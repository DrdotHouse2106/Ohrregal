# Privacy policy for Ohrregal

Updated: November 2026 · Deutsche Fassung (maßgeblich): [DATENSCHUTZ.md](DATENSCHUTZ.md)

Ohrregal is an app for your **own** servers (Navidrome and Audiobookshelf). The developer runs no servers for the app and receives no data from you.

- **Login data** (server address, user name, password) stays on your device; passwords in the iOS keychain.
- **Played tracks, positions, playlists and likes** go only to the servers you enter yourself.
- **Listening log:** Only if the optional Ohrregal service runs on your server, the app sends it what you play, skip, search and wish for. It is used for suggestions only and can be viewed and deleted in the app.
- **Song recognition:** Tapping "Erkennen" briefly uses the microphone and Apple's ShazamKit. No recording is stored.
- **Radio station search:** only the search term goes to the free directory radio-browser.info. Saved stations stay on the device.
- **Notifications:** If allowed, the app passes the push token to your own server.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account with the developer. No data is sold or shared.
